ACCURACY LEDGER (Phase 1, rebuild of round 1)

One entry per value change: id, what changed, a before -> after example with real numbers, the
owner decision behind it, and the test that fails before the change and passes after.
"Seeded" means the pricing every new database starts with (supabase/migrations), which equals the
live rows; tests use it through lib/pricing/__fixtures__/seeded.ts (seeded-rows.json; the money
stage used migrationSeed.ts, which the cavity stage replaced with it: the same values). "Hand fixture" is the
hand-made test pricing in lib/pricing/__fixtures__/seed.ts. Drive is 20 min one way unless stated.

=== Stage "money": the engine's money lines and option rules ===

L1  Each item is priced from its own cost, the same in every option; the rest of the price is a
    "Travel and setup" line.
    Each work item = its cost x (1 + overhead) / (1 - target margin), rounded down to the dollar.
    Travel and setup = the price the costs ask for minus the work items (drive, trips, the $5 rounding).
    Example (seeded): attic 1,200 sq ft at R-19, old insulation removed.
      Removal: Good $2,720.15 / Better $2,735.84 -> $2,483 in both.
      Good: $2,720.15 + $3,664.85 -> $2,483 + $3,345 + Travel and setup $557.
      Totals unchanged: Good $6,385, Better $8,010.
    Owner decision: OD2.
    Tests: lib/pricing/phase1.test.ts "the same removal costs the same in every option; travel and
    setup is its own line", "the same insulation and air sealing cost the same in Better and Best",
    property "the same work is the same price in every option, whatever the drive and the overrides".

L2  The minimum job top-up is its own "Minimum job charge" line.
    Example (seeded today): walls 1,000 sq ft, 2x4, Good: one item $1,500 -> $820 work + $190 travel
      and setup + $490 minimum job charge = $1,500.
    The record's example ($1,254 + $186 + $60) used the dense-pack wall bag (122.5 sq ft x in per
      bag, 31 bags) that the insulation stage adds (L20). With that coverage the engine gives exactly
      $1,254 + $186 + $60.
    After L20 (stage "cavity") the seeded example itself reads $1,254 + $186 + $60.
    Owner decision: OD2.
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "a job under the minimum shows its work, travel and the top-up to the
    minimum", "with the dense-pack wall coverage of 122.5 sq ft x in per bag, the record example
    reads $1,254 + $186 + $60".

L3  An estimator's price is a "Price adjustment" line; items keep their prices.
    Example (seeded): attic 900 sq ft at R-19, Better set to $3,000: items $2,373.44 + $626.56 ->
      $2,153 + $568 + travel and setup $374 + price adjustment -$95 (model price $3,095).
      A price raised by hand: attic 1,000, Good $3,100 -> adjustment +$105 over $2,995.
    Owner decision: OD3.
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "Better set to $3,000 keeps its items and shows the change", "a price
    raised by hand shows a positive adjustment".

L4  Printed whole-dollar items add up to the printed total.
    The record's example (garages 1,002 + 2,526 + crawlspace 368, Better, $14,994 under $14,995)
      came from a random quote whose other inputs are not in the record; at those sizes with plain
      inputs the Better total is $10,865, so it cannot be re-run.
    Example (seeded): garage 1,002 sq ft (difficult access) + crawlspace 368 sq ft, Best: printed
      $6,154 + $2,072 + $283 = $8,509 under an $8,510 total -> $5,482 + $1,846 + $252 + travel
      and setup $930 = $8,510.
    Owner decision: OD4.
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "printed items add up to the printed total (S1-F2)", property "every
    customer line is whole dollars, only the adjustment can be negative, and the lines add up".

L5  A price override rounds to whole dollars, and the price step is at least $1.
    Examples:
      Seeded attic 1,000 sq ft, Good set to $4,321.50: $4,321.50 -> $4,322 (adjustment +$1,327).
      Deviation note, hand fixture Better set to $7,224.50: "you are quoting $7,224.50" -> "$7,225".
      Price rounding set to 0 (seeded attic 1,000, Good): $2,990.92 -> $2,991.
      Price advice (win model) with rounding 0, $1,000 loaded cost: $1,333.34 / $1,335.48 ->
        $1,334 / $1,336, the same step as the engine.
    Owner decision: OD4.
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "a price change is whole dollars (S1-F1, OD4)", "the deviation note quotes
    the whole-dollar price", "the price step is at least $1: a rounding of 0 prices to the dollar";
    lib/learning/deviation.test.ts "quotes a price typed with cents in whole dollars, and says when
    there is no reason" (was "formats cents and a missing reason", $5,560.50 over $4,815 before the
    fixture change); lib/learning/winRate.test.ts "rounds up to whole dollars when rounding is off
    (as the engine does, L5), and includes a ceiling off the step grid".

L6  An override under $1 is ignored, with an override_too_low warning; Apply waits for $1 or more.
    Example (seeded): attic 900 sq ft, Good $0.40: printed $0 -> $2,735 (the model price) plus
      "Good price $0.40 is under $1, so it is not used. The price stays $2,735."
    Owner decision: R7 (review finding), OD4.
    Test: phase1.test.ts "a price under $1 is not used; the estimator is told (R7)".

L7  Install labor is priced for the quantity priced (the model's hours stay the model's).
    Hours priced = setup + (quantity / waste) x rate x access, only when the estimator changed the
    quantity; an estimator's own hours still win.
    Examples (hand fixture): Good blown fiberglass 58 -> 70 bags: labor 10 h -> 11.72 h priced
      (+$68.80); learning estHours 18.88 -> 19.81 for 95 bags (attic 1,000, difficult, Better).
      Seeded air sealing 1,000 -> 1,500 sq ft: 6.5 h -> 9.5 h.
    Owner decision: S1-F3 / R3.
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "more bags take more install hours; the model hours stay the model", "the
    learning loop reads the hours priced for the estimator's bags", "an air-sealing quantity moves
    the air-sealing labor the same way"; lib/learning/observations.test.ts "builds one observation
    per area x product with a priced line and an actual" (estHours 18.88 -> 19.81);
    lib/pricing/overrides.test.ts "a qty override on a material line" (direct cost +$456 -> +$524.80).

L14 Duplicates and options that do not step up are not shown, with an option_hidden warning.
    An option with the same work as another is shown once, at the lower price (the lower tier on a
    tie); then from the top down an option is shown only when the next shown option up costs at
    least $1 more. priceEstimate(input, snapshot, coeffs, { showAll: true }) returns every option.
    Examples (seeded):
      Attic 1,200 sq ft at R-0: Best $5,160 (same as Better) -> not shown, "Best is not shown to the
        customer: it is the same work as Better."
      Rim joist 150 sq ft, all three at the $1,500 minimum: Good, Better, Best shown -> Best only.
      Attic 1,000, Good set to $4,500 above Better $4,390: Good shown -> not shown, "... Change its
        price to show it."
      Quote document: a picked option that is not shown is no longer marked recommended
        ('best' -> 'better').
    Owner decision: OD6.
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "Best that repeats Better is not shown, and the estimator is told",
    "options that all cost the same show only the one that does the most", "an option the estimator
    priced above the next one up is hidden, and says how to show it", "of two options with the same
    work, the cheaper one is shown"; lib/pricing/engine.test.ts "priceEstimate - tier order (OD6)";
    engine.property.test.ts "OD6: the options shown step up ..."; lib/quotes/model.test.ts "never
    recommends an option the quote does not carry (OD6)".

L15 Titles and bullets come from the work actually priced.
    Examples (seeded):
      Attic at R-11, target R-30, Good: "Blown Cellulose to Code" -> "Blown Cellulose to R-30"; the
        bullet "Brings your attic to current code R-value" is removed.
      Attic at R-60, Better: "Air Seal + Blown Cellulose" -> "Air Sealing"; bullets -> only "Seals
        the leaks that drive drafts and high bills".
      Crawlspace 800 at R-19, Best: "Closed-Cell Foam + Air Sealing" -> "Air Sealing".
      Attic with nothing to remove, Best (showAll): "Remove, Air Seal + Cellulose" -> "Air Sealing +
        Blown Cellulose"; "Old, dirty or damaged insulation removed" dropped.
      Package (attic at R-60 + walls), Good: the attic's two bullets are no longer listed.
    Owner decision: S1-F7, R2.
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "a target below code is not called \"to Code\"", "an option that only air
    seals says so (R2, E2 and E3)", "\"Remove\" is said only when removal is priced", "a package only
    carries bullets of areas with work in it (R2)".

L16 Air-sealing words per job type.
    Example (seeded): crawlspace 800 sq ft, Best: "Air sealing of top plates, penetrations and
      bypasses (800 sq ft)" -> "Air sealing of the rim, penetrations and gaps in the floor above
      (800 sq ft)". Attics keep their words. Walls, rim joists, garages and commercial jobs have their
      own words, used only if the owner adds air sealing to one of their options.
    Owner decision: S3-F20.
    Test: phase1.test.ts "air sealing says where it is done (S3-F20)".

L17 Areas under 10 sq ft keep one decimal.
    Example (seeded): attic 0.3 sq ft: "0 sq ft" -> "0.3 sq ft" (12.4 sq ft still reads "12 sq ft").
    Owner decision: S3-F23.
    Test: phase1.test.ts "a tiny area never reads \"0 sq ft\" (S3-F23)".

Test data only (no customer price): the hand fixture's cellulose bag $14 -> $22 so its Good ($5,420)
stays below Better ($6,255) under OD6. Math tests price every option with showAll.

=== Stage "cavity": insulation that fits (L18-L24) ===

Seeded below includes the accuracy migration's rows (supabase/migrations/20261119000001_accuracy.sql):
the dense-pack wall bag, seven batt sizes and the walls cellulose waste cell. Easy access unless
stated. "Before" is the engine after the money stage.

L18 A wall with no depth entered is a 2x4 wall, a 3.5 in cavity; nothing is installed thicker than
    the cavity.
    Example (seeded): walls 1,000 sq ft, no depth, Better: "Open-Cell Spray Foam to R-20 (~5.4" added)"
      $3,580 -> "Open-Cell Spray Foam to R-13 (fills the 3.5 in cavity)" $2,520 (6,756.8 -> 4,375 board
      ft), with a warning "the 3.5 in cavity (no depth entered: a 2x4 wall) only fits R-13 of open-cell
      spray foam (target R-20)". Synthetic S1-F4 (moderate access): Better $3,795 -> $2,660; walls
      650 sq ft difficult: Better $2,810 -> $2,005; walls 2,400 no depth: Better $8,865 -> $5,770.
    The phone's depth hint for walls now says a wall with no depth is priced as a 2x4.
    Owner decision: OD5 (S1-F4, RF-08).
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "walls with no depth are priced as a 2x4 wall (S1-F4, OD5)"; guards "a depth
    entered is the cavity: a 2x6 wall takes R-20 of open-cell", "an open attic has no cavity".

L19 Insulation already in an area takes its room: its depth entered, else its R over the one
    R-per-inch table (L24); removed first, the whole cavity is free. With under 0.5 in of room left
    the cavity is full: that option adds no product there, with a cavity_full warning.
    Examples (seeded):
      Walls 1,000 sq ft, 2x4, R-11 fiberglass batts (3.44 in): Good $1,500 / Better $1,740 / Best
        $2,505 -> no options; three warnings "Walls: the 3.5 in cavity already holds 3.44 in of
        insulation, so Good adds no blown cellulose." (and Better, Best).
      Synthetic S1-F5, walls 800 sq ft, 2x4, 2 in of cellulose (R-7), moderate: Better $2,245 -> $1,500
        ("Open-Cell Spray Foam to R-12.6 (~1.5 in added, fills the 3.5 in cavity)", 1,500 board ft),
        Best $3,115 -> $2,295; Good ($1,500, the same as Better) is no longer shown (OD6).
    An existing material not named, not in the table, or "None" with an R is read at the highest R
    per inch (3.7), so the room left is never understated; the checker reads it the same way.
    Owner decision: OD5 (S1-F5).
    Tests: phase1.test.ts "existing insulation counts against the cavity (S1-F5): a full cavity gets
    nothing", "walls with 2 in of cellulose: the new insulation fills only the 1.5 in left", "removed
    first, the whole cavity is free again", "an existing material named in any case, or not named,
    takes room by the one table".
