ORIGINAL AUDIT SUMMARY (from the Koala Estimator Franchise Plan page, Nov 18 2026)

What the check found
 Accuracy and ease of use
 Every price, measurement and screen was checked by role: owner, office, estimator, crew and customer. Thousands of random quotes were run through the pricing engine. The real PDF, email and signing page were rendered and read.
 25 number problems 
 9 change what a customer sees or pays 
 Customer One price, three readings
 The same price shows as $4,322, $4,321.50 and $4,321.5. On the signing page the text says $4,321.50 and the button says $4,322.
 Customer Line items don't add up to the total
 In about 1 in 5 multi-line options. One real PDF lists items totaling $42,941 under "Best total: $42,940".
 Customer The same work costs different amounts in each option
 The same removal was $11,663 in Good and $10,730 in Better. This happens in 98% of quotes that share an item.
 Customer Insulation that can't fit
 A wall with no depth entered is quoted at R-20 with 5.6 in. of insulation, more than a 2×4 wall holds. Already-full cavities get more, and batts are never limited by the cavity.
 Customer Option names promise work that isn't priced
 "To Code" when the target is below code, "Remove…" with no removal. Better and Best come out identical whenever removal is needed.
 Customer "Valid until" is a day late
 The link expires a day before the date printed on the quote.
 Customer Dense-pack wall cellulose is about 57% short on bags
 The learning loop's limits stop it from correcting this on its own.
 Inside "Target margin" means something other than its label
 It is labeled gross but works as net after overhead. A new owner who types the usual 48% gross margin would overprice every quote by about 63%. Newark's prices are fine as set.
 Inside No sales tax
 Fine in Delaware. Most other states need it on the quote.
 Inside Also found
 A pitch typed as degrees is read as rise per 12 (+133% roof area). A broken measurement keeps the old square feet. Quote numbers will collide once there are many franchises. Phone and office lists can show different totals. In the satellite takeoff, carport parts are on by default and add about 270 sq ft.
 20 screen problems 
 4 would lose a demo 
 Demo risk Unfinished text on the customer's quote
 "Workmanship warranty — PLACEHOLDER" prints on a signed PDF. "We emailed you a copy" shows when email is off. "Call our office" gives no number.
 Demo risk The same quote has different statuses on different screens
 "Draft" next to "Quote v1 sent", "Sent" next to "declined", and a job badge that says Scheduled on a card that says "Not scheduled yet".
 Demo risk No shared menu, and no sign-out in the field and crew apps
 Three separate apps. Two pages are called Estimates and two are called Jobs.
 Demo risk Send is blocked by messages an estimator can't act on
 "Too small: expected number to be >0", with no link to the area that caused it.
 Ease of use Long, dense screens
 One area card runs up to 3,000 pixels tall on a phone. The owner's pricing page is about 12,500 pixels with about 25 Save buttons. The satellite sheet hides "Yes, this is the house" about 900 pixels down.
 Ease of use Jargon and internals on screen
 "Pricing v1 · ed7d0f4", "n_eff", "±SE", "MAPE", product codes. The Learning page reads like a statistics report.
 Ease of use No home screen for any role, and no search
 Every role lands on a plain list. Nothing says "needs you now".
 Ease of use Failures look like something else
 When the database is down, every page sends you to sign-in, which looks like being signed out. After a customer signs, the estimator's phone shows a false "changed on another device" warning.
 Built for one shop
 These are hard-coded today and become settings each franchise fills in:
 "Koala Insulation of Newark" in about 12 places 
 Teal brand color 
 "KN-" quote numbers 
 "Delaware UETA" signing text 
 Newark, DE address defaults 
 Delmarva and City of Newark utilities 
 Energize Delaware lead source 
 Climate zone 4A code minimums 
 New York time zone in 11+ places 
 Delaware labor and market prices 
 Newark demo data 
 One email sender and one set of API keys 
 Checked and correct
 Every quote total splits to the cent.
 Financing is rounded once and matches everywhere. A 0% APR works.
 No blank, infinite or negative numbers across 6 job types with extreme sizes, R-values and drive times.
 Attic floor and wall run are exact on a rectangle. Roofing squares and bundles round up correctly.
 The phone's price matches the server's to the cent when a quote is sent.
 All 934 automated tests pass.
