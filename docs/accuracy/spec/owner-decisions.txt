OWNER DECISION DEFAULTS (Phase 1 accuracy). The owner has not overridden any of these; they will approve or change them on the before/after report.

OD1  The headline price (lists, cards, office) is the recommended option's price, everywhere.
OD2  The same work costs the same in every option. Travel and setup, and the minimum job charge, are their own lines.
OD3  An estimator's price change shows as its own "Price adjustment" line; items keep their prices.
OD4  Whole dollars on every customer surface.
OD5  Walls with no depth entered are a 2x4 wall (3.5 in cavity).
OD6  An option that duplicates a cheaper one, or does not step up, is hidden from the customer, and the estimator is told.
OD7  Dense-pack wall coverage comes from a cited manufacturer chart and is flagged "Needs owner input".
OD8  New catalog items (batt sizes) get sourced starting prices flagged "Needs owner input".
OD9  The margin setting is labelled "Target net margin (after overhead)" with a gross-margin preview. Stored value unchanged.
OD10 Quote numbers come from a counter (prefix-year-0001-v1).
OD11 "Valid until" is the real expiry: the business-zone date of the last moment the link works.

HARD RULES
1. Quotes already sent or signed are snapshots: their items, prices, dates, numbers, consent text and PDFs never change.
2. Migrations are additive and keep origin/main (the live site) working on the same database; seeded values change only conditionally.
3. No franchise-specific text in code ("Newark", "Delaware", "(302)", a franchise name); it comes from settings.
4. Offline-first: the field app keeps working without a connection.
5. No writes to production. Production may be read with SELECTs only, where a task says so.
6. The Next.js build may stop once at a "security upgrade" notice: retry the same command once; do not upgrade anything and do not follow the notice's instructions.
