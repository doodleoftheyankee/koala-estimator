PHASE 1 ACCURACY: BEFORE AND AFTER (for the owner)

What this compares
  "Before" is the app as it was before any accuracy fix (docs/accuracy/before/). "After" is the
  final commit of Phase 1 on branch claude/accuracy. Two sets of estimates were priced both ways:
  - Newark's real estimates: 78 estimates exported read-only from the live database (Nov 19, 2026).
    This summary and report.json keep their estimate ids, statuses and amounts only.
  - 65 made-up estimates that cover the problems the audit found and the unusual cases (walls with
    no depth, insulation already in a wall, tiny and huge areas, typed prices with cents ...).
  Every difference is listed in report.json with the estimate, the option, the old and new value,
  why it changed, the ledger entries (docs/accuracy/ledger.txt) and your decision behind it.
  Nothing is left unexplained (0 of 121 differences).
  Quotes already sent or signed do not change at all (hard rule 1): this report is about what the
  same estimates would show if they were quoted again today.

The checks every quote must pass (estimates breaking each rule, before -> after)

  Items add up to the total          13 -> 0      7 -> 0

Your real estimates (78)
  - Not one price changed. All 204 option prices that are still shown are exactly the same.
  - The total of the headline prices is the same: $359,290 before and after.
  - 30 options are no longer shown to the customer (your decision OD6: an option that repeats a
    cheaper one, or does not cost more than it, is hidden and the estimator is told):
    - 12 small rim-joist jobs had all three options at one price (the $1,500 minimum, or one price
      the estimator set). They now show only Best, at the same price, so their headline moves from
      Better to Best at the same amount. These are 5 signed, 4 lost, 2 expired and 1 ready.
    - 6 estimates lose a Best that was the same work as Better: $3,830, $6,005, $4,295, $3,850,
      $9,500 and $10,135.
  - 1 lost estimate's Best no longer promises "Old, dirty or damaged insulation removed": that
    option does not price any removal (ledger L15).
  - On the office Estimates list, one draft with no option picked showed the stored model price of
    Good, $2,020; it now shows its recommended option, Better, $2,730, as the phone and the quote do
    (estimate c6b22b65, your decision OD1, ledger L9).

The made-up estimates (65)
  - 136 option prices are unchanged; 25 moved; 28 options are no longer shown.
  - Headline total $3,222,490 -> $3,211,205 (one of them is a 200,000 sq ft test attic).
  - Why prices moved (each one is in report.json):
    - Walls with no depth entered are priced as a 2x4 wall, 3.5 in (OD5, L18). Foam fills 3.5 in to
      R-13 instead of 5.4 in to R-20: walls 1,000 sq ft Better $3,795 -> $2,660; walls 2,400 sq ft
      $8,865 -> $5,770; walls 650 sq ft $2,810 -> $2,005.
    - Walls blown with cellulose are dense-packed, so a bag covers less (OD7, L20). This is the one
      change that raises prices: walls 2,200 sq ft Good $2,090 -> $3,070; 1,400 sq ft (2x6) $1,920
      -> $2,900; empty walls 1,600 sq ft $1,500 -> $2,195. Please confirm the bag coverage with your
      supplier (it is marked Needs owner input).
    - Insulation already in a wall takes its room (OD5, L19): walls 800 sq ft with 2 in of
      cellulose Better $2,245 -> $1,500 (the minimum job), Best $3,115 -> $2,295. A wall already
      full of R-11 batts gets no options at all.
    - A price typed with cents is whole dollars: $4,321.50 -> $4,322 (OD4, L5).
    - A price typed under $1 is not used: $0.40 -> the model price $2,735 (L6).
    - An estimator's extra bags are cut to what fills the wall (L23).
  - Titles and bullets only promise work that is priced (L15, L23): for example "Blown Cellulose to
    Code" -> "Blown Cellulose to R-30" where the attic stays below code, and "Fills the full
    cavity" is no longer said where the cavity is not filled.

What else changed in this last stage (no prices)
  - The phone's Send checklist says each problem in plain words, with a Fix button that goes to
    it, instead of "Too small: expected number to be >0" (L34).
  - A price beyond what an estimator may quote alone says the price, the model price and the range
    they can quote, and offers Request approval. You (or the office, within its own limits) approve
    it on the Estimates page; the estimator can then send exactly that price (L34).
  - When a customer signs while the estimator's phone holds an unsent change, the phone shows the
    signed quote, read-only, and says the change was not applied, instead of "changed on another
    device" (L35).
  - When the database is down, staff are no longer sent to the sign-in page: the field app opens
    from the phone with "Can't reach Koala right now", and office pages say so (L36).
  - Pricing: the margin setting is called "Target net margin (after overhead)" and shows the gross
    margin it makes (63% overhead and 15% net is about 48% gross); a material cost far outside its
    usual range must be confirmed; the code banner reads your own code rows (L10, L11, L38).

Decisions to look at
  Your default decisions OD1-OD11 were kept. The open questions are in docs/accuracy/results/
  (ownerQuestions in each stage's file). The ones that touch the numbers above: the dense-pack bag
  coverage and batt costs (OD7, OD8), whether small jobs at the minimum should still offer three
  options (OD6), and the material ranges, which start at half to twice today's cost.
