How the code keeps it
  A document now carries `format: 2` (lib/quotes/model.ts DOCUMENT_FORMAT) and its own consent
  sentence. A stored document has neither, so isLegacyDocument() is true for it and:
    - sentences print the price with cents (sentenceMoney), as before; new quotes print whole
      dollars (L8);
    - the expired page says its printed "Valid until" date (expiredOn); new quotes say the moment
      the link stopped (L13);
    - it is signed under the sentence it was sent with (consentFor -> LEGACY_ESIGN_CONSENT, word for
      word), and the e-sign record stores that sentence (L31);
    - its PDF says "Sign online at the link in your email." and lays out as before (no
      keep-together, and no wrap prop at all, since react-pdf lays out `wrap={undefined}`
      differently from none) (L33, L39);
    - its number, dates and items are read from the document, never recomputed (L12, L13).
  What is not part of a sent document follows the new rules for every quote: the thank-you page
  says the copy was emailed only when it was (L33), and the office list's headline is the
  recommended option (L9).

To repeat it
  1. Export: run scripts/accuracy/quote-docs-export.sql read-only (SELECT only) and save the `doc`
     column as {"quotes": [...]} (check its md5).
  2. node scripts/accuracy/render-run.mjs scripts/accuracy/render-stored.ts <docs.json> <out-before>
     on the code before the change (a git worktree), and again with <out-after> on the change.
  3. diff -r <out-before> <out-after>
