BLOWER DOOR AIR SEAL CALCULATOR IN KOALA (branch claude/blower-door, from claude/daymark)
=====================================================================================

What the owner asked for: their single-page Blower Door Air Seal Calculator, inside Koala, for
every franchise. It keeps the tool's behaviour and words; the rebate programs became franchise
settings; readings can be saved to a job or an estimate, offline too; the office sees them.


1. THE ARITHMETIC (lib/blowerdoor/, pure, no React)
---------------------------------------------------
  numbers.ts    reading a typed field: "$", commas, spaces and the sign dropped (a gauge shows
                negative pressure; a lone "-" is not typed yet, not an error); empty, zero,
                nonsense and absurd values come back as an error in words (ranges: fan flow
                0-50,000 CFM, pressure 1-100 Pa, exponent 0.5-1.0 with at most 3 decimals, price
                over $0, BAS 1-100,000, volume 500-1,000,000 ft3, occupants 0-50 whole, N-factor
                1-40). Values are read at the precision a saved reading keeps (CFM and Pa 2
                decimals, BAS and volume 1, N-factor 2, price cents), so what the screen works out
                is what is saved. Below 25 Pa the screen notes that the CFM50 is stretched far.
  calc.ts       CFM50 = CFM x (50 / Pa)^n; reduction %; tiers (reached when post50 <=
                pre50 x (1 - pct/100), exactly on the limit counts, with each tier's limit, the
                next tier's gap rounded up and the top tier's spare rounded down); the cost cap
                (tier amount or cap % of the price, whichever is lower, floored to the cent);
                BAS from the audit (preferred) or max(0.35 x volume / 60, 15 x occupants) x
                N-factor x height correction (1, .89, .81, .76, .72); ventilation OK at or above
                the BAS, RECOMMEND from 70%, INSTALL under 70%, with the continuous fan flow in
                CFM (gap / (N x height correction); the whole requirement when it must be
                installed); ACH50. Nothing returns NaN or Infinity: a reading it cannot use is
                null.
  settings.ts   the franchise settings (app_settings.blower_door): programs (key, full name,
                short name, tiers, cost cap %, mechanical ventilation rebate cap, source,
                effective date, paperwork list and its source), the gauge name, the default
                flow exponent and N-factor, and whether the tool decides on whole-number CFM50
                (whole_cfm50, off unless the owner turns it on). A lenient reader for the tool (a broken program is
                left out and named) and a strict validator for saving (tiers ascending, a higher
                tier never pays less, whole dollars, percentages between 0 and 100, unique keys
                and short names, real dates).
  evaluate.ts   everything the screen shows, from what was typed, the readings already saved
                and the chosen program. A saved reading is used as recorded: its CFM50 is never
                worked out again with a later exponent.
  words.ts      the headline ("40% TIER — $1,800 AIR SEAL REBATE" / "BELOW 20% — NO AIR SEAL
                REBATE YET" / "TEST-IN ONLY" / "NO REBATE PROGRAM SET UP"), the next-tier line,
                the ventilation messages, the N-factor hint, the source line and the copied
                summary, in the owner's words. shownNumbers(): every CFM50, tier limit, BAS and
                reduction on screen agrees with the decision (see 7, BD-MATH-3).
  record.ts     the saved reading (what the phone sends, what the server returns) and the job's
                result from its newest test-in and test-out, with the program saved with them,
                the price from the newest reading saved with one, and a warning when the test-in
                and the test-out name different programs; savedProgramKey() for the phone.
  Tests: blowerdoor.test.ts, settings.test.ts, record.test.ts, franchise.test.ts (no program,
  sponsor, state or dollar amount in the tool's code).

  The worked example: 6,952 CFM at 50 Pa in, 3,325 CFM at 50.8 Pa out, n 0.65 -> 3,291 CFM50,
  52.7%, the 40% tier, $1,800 on Home Performance ($2,000 Assisted).


2. FRANCHISE SETTINGS, NOT CODE
-------------------------------
  app_settings.blower_door holds the programs. This franchise's two programs (Home Performance
  with ENERGY STAR and Assisted Home Performance, tiers 20/30/40% = $900/$1,300/$1,800 and
  $1,100/$1,500/$2,000, 75% cost cap, ventilation rebate up to $400/$600, schedule effective
  2024-11-01, the four paperwork items) are seeded by the migration, only when the setting is
  absent. The owner edits them on Rebate programs (/admin/rebates, admin menu; the office sees it
  read-only), checked as typed and again on save, audited. With no program set up the tool still
  converts and shows the reduction and the ventilation check, and says "No rebate program set
  up" (the owner gets a "Set up programs" button, others are told to ask the owner).


3. THE FIELD TOOL (/field/blower-door, Daymark field register)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  app/field/blower-door/page.tsx, BlowerDoorTool.tsx, parts.tsx, BlowerDoorLink.tsx.
  Owner, office, estimator and crew can open it. In lib/nav.ts beside Roof lookup ("Blower
  door"; a tab for crews, under More for the others). The result plate (big gauge, the tier in
  words, a bar with a tick per tier), program buttons, test-in and test-out cards with 64px
  gauge fields, the tier table, the cost cap (not shown to crews: they never type a price; they
  are told the office applies the cap), the ventilation check with BAS from audit or the
  estimate and the Advanced flow exponent, the numbers, the paperwork with the source line, and
  a dock with Copy summary and Clear for new job.
  Offline: the page is its own price-free service worker shell (public/sw.js VERSION v6, SHELLS
  includes /field/blower-door); the programs are cached on the phone by the field app's and the
  crew app's sync cycles (like the pricing bundle) and by the tool itself; what was typed is
  kept per person and per job, estimate or the plain calculator (IndexedDB 'koala-blower-door').

  Saved to the job: opened from the estimate (Areas step of the estimate editor) or from a crew
  job (crew app), each test has a Save button. A saved reading shows as recorded (CFM50, the
  reading, n, who and when, and whether it is on the server or still on the phone); Retake types
  a new one, and the newest counts. The house (BAS inputs) given with a saved reading fills the
  crew's empty fields, so the estimator's volume reaches the test-out.


4. SAVED READINGS
-----------------
  supabase/migrations/20261120000001_blower_door.sql (additive; sorts after
  20261119000001_accuracy.sql): table blower_door_tests (estimate_id, job_id, kind, cfm, pa,
  exponent, cfm50, program_key, program as it was, air_sealing_cost, bas_audit, volume,
  occupants, stories, n_factor, result as shown, recorded_by, recorded_at). The CFM50 must be
  the reading at its stored exponent (a check constraint); a job's reading takes the job's
  estimate; no update or delete policy, so readings stay as recorded; audited.
  Who sees: owner and office all; an estimator their own estimates' readings; a crew lead only
  their crew's jobs and the test-in taken on the estimate the job came from, and only through
  blower_door_context(), which leaves the air sealing price out (the table itself is not theirs
  to read). Who records: the same, only through sync_blower_door_test() (the outbox; idempotent
  on the mutation id and on the reading's own id; dated no later than now; it keeps the
  franchise's own program from app_settings by its key, never the copy the phone sent). No
  direct insert, update or delete. blower_door_context() gives the tool a job's or estimate's
  label and readings. Crews may read the one setting row app_settings.blower_door and no
  other.
  API: GET /api/blower-door/settings, GET/POST /api/blower-door/tests.
  Phone: lib/offline/blowerDoor.ts (Dexie store and outbox) and blowerDoorCore.ts (pure rules,
  tested). The tool keeps its own small IndexedDB database because the estimator's field app and
  the crew app each have their own and the tool serves both; the outbox is sent by both apps'
  existing sync cycles (lib/offline/sync.ts syncAll, lib/offline/crewSync.ts crewSyncAll) and
  uses the same idempotency ledger on the server (sync_mutations). The crew job card and the
  estimate's card load the saved readings onto the phone while there is signal, so the
  estimator's test-in is there when the crew opens the tool in an attic with none. A reading taken with no signal waits on the phone and syncs on the next field-app or
  crew-app sync (or the tool's own), once; one taken on an estimate that has not synced yet waits
  for it; a refused one (not the person's job right now) stays on the phone with the reason and
  is sent again on later syncs, in case the office gives the job back; one with values the
  server never takes stays "Not saved". The copied summary says when a reading in it has not
  reached the office. Values are rounded to what the database keeps before the CFM50 is worked
  out. Save is off while a save is in flight, so a double tap stores one reading. Until someone
  picks a program, the tool uses the program the job's saved readings name ("Chosen when the
  test-in was saved."), so the estimator's choice at the audit reaches the crew's test-out.
  Office job page (app/jobs/[id]/BlowerDoorCard.tsx): the result plate, the test-in and
  test-out with who took them and their program, the reduction, the tier and rebate (with the
  cost cap from the newest price saved with any reading, so the estimator's price caps the
  crew's test-out), a warning when the readings name different programs, the ventilation
  check, ACH50, and every reading with its program and Newest / Earlier.
  tests/sql/50_blower_door.test.sql covers seeding, access, idempotency, the checks and that
  nobody edits or deletes a reading.

6. QUESTIONS FOR THE OWNER
--------------------------
  The current behaviour is kept for each of these until you answer; where it can be changed in
  the settings, it says where.
  a. Office role: the brief named estimator, crew lead and owner. The office also has the tool
     in its menu (it already has Roof lookup, and can open it from the office job page). Say if
     the office should not have it.
  b. Mistaken readings are never edited or deleted (a retest supersedes them, and the office
     page lists the earlier ones). Should the owner be able to delete one?
  c. The cost cap needs the installed air sealing price. Crews never type it. The office page
     now takes it from the newest reading saved with a price (normally the estimator's, with
     the test-in). Should it come from the signed quote's air sealing line instead, or from a
     field on the office job page?
  d. Rounding (BD-MATH-3): does your program decide the air sealing tier on the whole-number
     CFM50 of the Test Out Form and the gauge photo (6,952 then 5,562 = 19.99%, no rebate)? The
     tool decides on the exact converted CFM50 (as your original calculator did) and shows
     numbers that agree with that. If the program uses whole numbers, turn on "Decide on
     whole-number CFM50" on Rebate programs.
  e. Which program should a job use: the one the estimator chose at the audit? The crew's phone
     now starts on the program saved with the test-in, and the crew can still change it (the
     office page then warns that the readings name different programs and uses the
     test-out's). Should crews be able to change it at all?
  f. N-factor (BD-MATH-7): does your audit software report the zone's 1-story N-factor, or one
     already corrected for the number of stories? The tool asks for the 1-story value and
     applies the stories correction itself. The "Zone 2 runs 17–20" range could not be
     confirmed (LBL's table for zone 2, 1-story, runs from 16.7 to 22.2); the default is a
     setting on Rebate programs.
  g. Ventilation standard: Energize Delaware's Test-Out Form (Sept 2025) also asks for "Whole
     Building Ventilation per ASHRAE 62.2-2013", and current BPI practice (BPI-1200) uses
     ASHRAE 62.2. Should the tool also compute 62.2-2013, or is the 62-89 BAS check enough?
  h. Occupants: BPI's worked example uses the actual number of people, as the tool does;
     ASHRAE 62.2 uses bedrooms + 1. Does your program or audit software use "actual occupants
     or bedrooms + 1, whichever is greater"?
  i. Is the 11/01/2024 schedule still current? No newer sheet was found. The Air Sealing / Heat
     Pump bundling bonus ($300 / $350 when air sealing follows a heat pump within 3 months with
     at least a 20% reduction) is not in the tool. Do you want it?
  j. Is the mechanical ventilation rebate ($400 / $600) also capped at a share of the installed
     cost, like air sealing? The tool shows it only as "up to".
  k. Crews see rebate dollar amounts on the blower door page (the tier table and "40% TIER —
     $2,000 AIR SEAL REBATE"); the rest of the crew app shows no money, and crews never get the
     air sealing price. Is that all right, or should crews see only the tier reached?
  l. Any member of the job's crew can record readings (crew_members.is_lead is not checked, the
     same as for actuals). Should only crew leads save the test-out?
  m. A higher tier paying less than a lower one is refused when saving programs. Say if any
     program you work with does that.


7. REVIEW FIXES
---------------
  Each finding was reproduced first, a test written that failed on the old code, then fixed.
  "Before" numbers come from a scratch copy of ca0eb3b built and run on the e2e stack, with a
  probe (POSTs as the estimator and crew, Playwright for the screens).

  BD-MATH-1 / BD-R3 (price with the test-in ignored). Reproduced: estimator test-in under
    Assisted with $1,999, crew test-out with no price: the office showed "40% TIER — $1,800".
    Fix: jobBlowerDoor() takes the price from the newest reading saved with one (savedPrice()).
    Now "40% TIER — $1,499.25 AIR SEAL REBATE" with "(limited by 75% of $1,999)".
    Tests: record.test.ts, e2e (office step).
  BD-MATH-2 / BD-R2 (program not carried over). Reproduced: the crew's phone opened on Home
    Performance and saved the test-out under it. Fix: the tool uses savedProgramKey() until
    someone picks a program, and says so; it warns when the test-in names another program; the
    office page shows each reading's program and warns when the test-in and test-out differ
    (the result still uses the test-out's program; owner question e).
    Tests: record.test.ts, e2e (crew opens on Assisted, $2,000; saved as assisted).
  BD-MATH-3 (rounded display against the decision). Reproduced with unit tests: target 5,562,
    a 5,562 test-out shown "20.0%" beside "BELOW 20%", 5,561.55 and 5,562 both shown as 5,562
    with opposite results, 699.6 shown as 700 under "Under 70%", a "0 CFM50 gap". Fix:
    shownNumbers() and reductionText(): a limit is the largest whole CFM50 that reaches it
    (5,561); the reduction never rounds across a tier line (19.9%); where whole numbers would
    contradict a decision, the test-out and that line show a tenth, rounded toward the side
    the decision put them on (5,561.5 against 5,561.6; 699.6; a 0.3 CFM50 gap). The decision
    stays on the exact CFM50 (the original calculator's rule); the owner can switch to
    whole-number decisions on Rebate programs (owner question d).
    Tests: blowerdoor.test.ts (6,952/5,562; 5,576 at 50.2 Pa; 5,000/3,002; BAS 1,000 against
    699.6 and 999.7; the whole-number setting), settings.test.ts, record.test.ts, e2e.
  BD-MATH-4 (fan sizing in CFM). Reproduced: BPI's Albany example said "70 CFM50 gap" only.
    Fix: the ventilation words and the summary add "(about 4.5 CFM of continuous fan flow)" =
    gap / (N x height correction), and "(about 102 CFM ...)" for the whole requirement when it
    must be installed. BPI's sentence is kept. Tests: blowerdoor.test.ts (Albany numbers).
  BD-MATH-5 (office default never reaches a kept form). Reproduced: a kept form stayed at 0.65
    after the default became 0.6. Fix: the exponent and N-factor start empty and show the
    office default as a placeholder; a typed value is kept. (Forms kept on a phone by the v6
    preview still hold the old copy; clearing the field returns to the default.)
    Tests: blowerdoor.test.ts.
  BD-MATH-6 (exponent decimals). Reproduced: 0.6525 showed 6,288 and saved 6,290. Fix: a
    fourth decimal is refused ("Flow exponent: at most 3 decimals.", also for the setting);
    other fields are read at the saved precision. Tests: blowerdoor.test.ts, settings.test.ts,
    blowerDoorCore.test.ts (the CFM50 saved is the one shown). One existing test typed
    0.6543 to check rounding on save; it types 0.654 now (0.6543 is refused, tested apart).
  BD-MATH-7 (N-factor hint). Fix: the hint asks for the zone's 1-story value, says the tool
    applies the stories correction, and to set Stories to 1 if the software's N already allows
    for them; the same on Rebate programs. Tests: blowerdoor.test.ts (nFactorHint).
  BD-R1 (crews could read the price). Reproduced: the crew's GET returned air_sealing_cost 1999
    and PostgREST returned the rows with prices and the phone's result. Fix: the table's read
    policy is for owner, office and estimators; crews read through blower_door_context(), which
    leaves the price out. Tests: 50_blower_door.test.sql, e2e (crew's copy has no price).
  BD-R4 (service worker). Reproduced in a unit test that runs public/sw.js in a stand-in
    worker: estimator, then crew, then the blower door tool, then offline /field gave the
    estimator's /field shell. Fix: only /field and /crew count as the last shell; VERSION v7.
    Tests: lib/offline/serviceWorker.test.ts, e2e (a new step with a fresh phone).
  BD-R5 (direct inserts). Reproduced: a crew POST to /rest/v1/blower_door_tests dated 2099 with
    a made-up program got 201, and the office showed "1% TIER — $99,999 AIR SEAL REBATE". Fix:
    no insert policy (the outbox is the only way in, dated no later than now); the outbox keeps
    the franchise's own program by key, never the phone's copy (none for a key the franchise
    does not have). Tests: 50_blower_door.test.sql. The trigger and CFM50 checks in that file
    now run on an insert by the database owner (the outbox's path), since users cannot insert.
  BD-R6 (double tap). Reproduced: two clicks before the screen updated stored 2 extra
    readings. Fix: Save is off ("Saving…") until the saved reading is on screen.
    Tests: e2e (the estimator's Save test-in clicked twice in one go stores one reading).
  BD-R7 (refused never retried). Fix: "refused" is its own state, sent again on later syncs
    (the server keeps no record of a refusal); "invalid" stays as it was; the summary notes a
    reading that has not reached the office. Tests: blowerDoorCore.test.ts (the old assertion
    that refused became "invalid" and was never sent again now expects the retry).
  BD-R8 (lone minus sign). Fix: "-", "+", "." and "-." read as empty. The old test listed "-"
    among invalid inputs; it is now tested as empty, the rest stay invalid. Tests:
    blowerdoor.test.ts, e2e.
  BD-R9 (raw colour). Fix: the heading uses text-ink; screens.test.ts keeps raw palette colours
    out of the blower door screens.
  Existing tests changed because they encoded a reported bug: 20.0% beside BELOW 20% (now
  19.9%), the 5,562 target (now 5,561), the defaults copied into a new form (now empty), "-"
  as invalid, refused readings never retried, and the e2e scenario (the estimator now saves the
  test-in under Assisted with a $1,999 price, so the crew sees $2,000 and the office $1,499.25
  instead of $1,800). Nothing was deleted or skipped.
