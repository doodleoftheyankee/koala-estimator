KOALA: EVERY OPEN QUESTION FOR THE OWNER, IN ONE PLACE (claude/release, Nov 19 2026)

This is the one list of questions still waiting for you. It replaces looking through the accuracy
round's results files (final.json, flow.json, quotes.json, fixa.json, fixb-screens.json), the blower
door notes (docs/blower-door/build.txt section 6) and docs/release/release.txt section 7: every
question still open there is here once, in plain words. Questions your decisions OD12-OD16 already
answered are listed at the end, so you can see where they went.

None of these stops the release. Until you answer, the app keeps doing what each question says it
does today. Where you can change something yourself in the app, it says where.


A. QUOTES AND SIGNING
---------------------
A1. New York homes: which law does the signature sentence name?
    New York never passed the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act ("UETA"), which the sentence
    names for every other state. Until review C, a New York quote said "(ESIGN Act and New York
    UETA)", a law that does not exist. A new New York quote now says "(ESIGN Act and the New York
    Electronic Signatures and Records Act)", New York's own law. Is that right, or should New York
    quotes use the general wording ("ESIGN Act and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act"), or
    should a lawyer word it? Quotes already sent keep the sentence they were sent with.
    (Was quotes.json; dropped from final.json; review C DOC-2 and CLAIM-1.)

A2. The signature sentence for a home with no state.
    A property with no real US state, and no shop address the app can read, gets "ESIGN Act and the
    Uniform Electronic Transactions Act" with no state, as your decision OD14 says. Should a lawyer
    confirm that wording? (final.json 6)

A3. The 16 older quotes whose "Valid until" date is one day late.
    Sixteen quotes sent before this work print a "Valid until" date one day after their link really
    stops working. They are kept exactly as sent (quotes already sent never change). Keep them as
    they are, or keep those 16 links open until the end of the printed day?
    (quotes.json 5, flow.json 4)

A4. Town and state an owner saved on the old business form.
    New estimates now start in the town and state of the shop address (Pricing page), as OD14 says,
    and ignore a town or state typed on the old business form. Your database has neither saved. Is
    that all right for another franchise that did save them? (final.json 7)

A5. The seeded shop address for a new franchise.
    A brand-new database starts with the shop address "Newark, DE 19711", so its new estimates start
    in Newark, DE until its owner changes the shop address on Pricing. Should a new franchise start
    with a blank shop address instead? (final.json 8)

A6. Who may change the optional Business profile page?
    Only the owner can change it; the office can look but not edit. Keep it that way? (quotes.json 6)


B. PRICES, MATERIALS AND OPTIONS
--------------------------------
B1. Dense-pack wall coverage (decision OD7, marked "Needs owner input").
    Cellulose blown into walls is dense-packed, so a bag covers less. The app uses a
    manufacturer's chart for 16 in on-center studs (122.5 sq ft x in per bag). Should it use the
    24 in on-center figure (118.3) instead? And is the bag 25 lb, as the material's name says?
    (final.json 4, flow.json 4)

B2. Batt prices (decision OD8, marked "Needs owner input").
    The seven batt sizes (R-11 to R-30) have sourced starting costs. Please check each one on the
    Pricing page against what you pay. (flow.json 4)

B3. Small jobs at the minimum charge (decision OD6).
    When a small job is lifted to the minimum job charge, Good, Better and Best can come to the
    same price; the copies are not shown, so the customer may see only Best. Should a small job
    still offer three different options? (flow.json 4)

B4. Usual cost ranges for materials.
    Each material has a "usual" cost range used to catch typing mistakes. The ranges start at
    half to twice today's cost. Replace them with your suppliers' real ranges on the Pricing page.
    (flow.json 3)

B5. "This cost is right" widens the usual range for good.
    When someone confirms a cost outside the usual range, the range now grows to include it (for
    example $0.06-$0.30), so a later typo up to that cost is no longer caught. Keep that, or should
    the confirmation count only for that exact cost? (final.json 10)

B6. Removing old insulation in an option.
    When an option would remove what is there and leave the area with less R than it has now, the
    option keeps what is there (it is then usually the same as Better and not shown). Keep that, or
    leave such an option out entirely? (final.json 1)

B7. Attic joist depth.
    Should the depth of an attic floor's joists ever limit how much loose fill an option blows in?
    The phone no longer sends a depth for attics. (final.json 2)

B8. How an R-value that fills the cavity is written.
    When a wall cavity limits the R-value, it is rounded half up (a 2x4 of open-cell foam reads
    R-13, the exact figure is 12.9). Should it always be rounded down, so it never reads above what
    the cavity holds? (final.json 3)

B9. A quantity of 0.
    A quantity of 0 now means nothing is installed. Material already on site is a price change, and
    the phone says so. Keep? (final.json 5)

B10. Which option is recommended when the picked one is hidden.
    Your decision OD15 covers a hidden Better. The same rule is used for a hidden Good or Best pick
    too (the shown option doing the same work, else the nearest above, else below); it chose the
    same option as the old rule in every quote checked. Keep it for all three, or only for Better?
    (final.json 9)

B11. Price approvals: what may the office approve?
    The office may approve a price within its own limits (never below the floor margin); the owner
    and the office decide. Keep? (flow.json 1)

B12. An approval belongs to the price it was given for.
    An approval is tied to the model price too, so if the estimate is re-priced it needs a new
    approval. Keep (the safer choice)? (flow.json 2)


C. BLOWER DOOR AND REBATES
--------------------------
C1. The rebate programs a new franchise starts with.
    The blower door setup gives every new database this franchise's two Energize Delaware programs
    until its owner edits or removes them on Rebate programs. Keep that, or should a new franchise
    start with no program? (release.txt 7a)

C2. The gauge name.
    Before a phone has its settings, the tool says "Retrotec Model 5000". Keep it, or say "the
    gauge" until the franchise sets one? (release.txt 7b)

C3. Rebates rounded down to the dollar.
    A capped rebate is rounded down to whole dollars ($1,499 for 75% of $1,999). If your program
    pays to the cent and you want cents shown, say so. (release.txt 7c)

C4. Where the air sealing price for the cost cap should come from.
    The office job page takes it from the newest reading saved with a price (normally the
    estimator's, with the test-in). Crews never type it. When no reading has a price, the page now
    says "up to $900 (before the 75% cost cap: no air sealing price saved)" instead of a bare $900.
    Should the price come from the signed quote's air sealing line instead, or from a field on the
    office job page? (build.txt c; review C FLOW-1)

C5. What crews see.
    Crews see rebate amounts on the blower door page; the rest of the crew app shows no money.
    With no price on the crew's phone, the amount now reads "UP TO $2,000" and the copied summary
    "rebate up to $2,000 (before the 75% cost cap)". Is that all right, or should crews see only
    the tier reached? (build.txt k; review C DOC-4)

C6. The office and the blower door tool.
    The office also has the tool in its menu. Say if it should not. (build.txt a)

C7. Mistaken readings.
    Readings are never edited or deleted; a new test replaces them, and the office page lists the
    earlier ones. Should the owner be able to delete one? (build.txt b)

C8. Whole-number CFM50.
    Does your program decide the tier on the whole-number CFM50 of the Test Out Form? The tool
    decides on the exact figure. If the program uses whole numbers, turn on "Decide on whole-number
    CFM50" on Rebate programs. (build.txt d)

C9. Which program a job uses.
    The crew's phone starts on the program saved with the test-in, and the crew can change it (the
    office page then warns). Should crews be able to change it at all? (build.txt e)

C10. N-factor.
    Does your audit software give the zone's 1-story N-factor, or one already corrected for
    stories? The tool asks for the 1-story value. The default is a setting on Rebate programs.
    (build.txt f)

C11. Ventilation standard.
    Should the tool also work out ASHRAE 62.2-2013 ventilation, or is the 62-89 BAS check enough?
    (build.txt g)

C12. Occupants.
    The tool uses the actual number of people. Does your program use "occupants or bedrooms + 1,
    whichever is greater"? (build.txt h)

C13. The 11/01/2024 rebate schedule.
    Is it still current? The air sealing / heat pump bundling bonus is not in the tool; do you want
    it? (build.txt i)

C14. The mechanical ventilation rebate.
    Is it also capped at a share of the installed cost? The tool shows it only as "up to".
    (build.txt j)

C15. Who may save readings.
    Any member of the job's crew can record readings. Should only crew leads save the test-out?
    (build.txt l)

C16. Tiers that pay less.
    A higher tier paying less than a lower one is refused when saving programs. Say if a program you
    work with does that. (build.txt m)


D. FRANCHISES AND GOING LIVE
----------------------------
D1. Each franchise's time zone.
    Every date and time the app prints is in one time zone per franchise. It is New York time
    unless the franchise's deployment sets NEXT_PUBLIC_BUSINESS_TIME_ZONE (for example
    America/Chicago or America/Los_Angeles) before it is built; a quote keeps the zone it was sent
    in. Should a franchise owner be able to choose the time zone on a page in the app instead?
    (review C DOC-1, FLOW-7)

D2. The Next.js security upgrade.
    The build shows a Next.js security notice. Nothing was upgraded in this work, as you asked. The
    upgrade is your call. (flow.json 5)
