Why the release cannot change a stored quote
  - Every stored quote is a document sent before the accuracy round: no format, no zone, no consent
    of its own. Review C's quote changes all go through what such a document already has:
      * its dates print in documentTimeZone(doc), which is America/New_York for a document without a
        zone: the zone every quote printed in before, whatever NEXT_PUBLIC_BUSINESS_TIME_ZONE says;
      * its expired page keeps the printed "Valid until" date (isLegacyDocument), with no zone name;
      * its PDF keeps exactly the old element tree: the new page-break blocks (DOC-5) and the kept
        sections apply to documents sent from the accuracy round on only; a unit test
        (lib/quotes/quotes-stage.test.ts, "a quote sent before the accuracy round lays out exactly
        as it did") compares their page text with a recording made on the code before the change;
      * it signs under its own sentence or the legacy one (consentFor); esignConsent (New York's
        own act) only words new quotes.
  - The blower door migration's one change in review C (sync_blower_door_test drops a crew's
    price) touches readings, not quotes. The accuracy migration is unchanged.
  - The accuracy migration's conditional seed replacements apply only to rows that still equal the
    seed, and a stored quote reads its company, terms, warranty, consent, number and dates from its
    own document (docs/accuracy/final/hard-rule-1.txt).
