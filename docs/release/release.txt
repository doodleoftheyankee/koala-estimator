KOALA RELEASE CANDIDATE: claude/release (Nov 19 2026)

claude/release is the release candidate for every Koala Insulation franchise: the accuracy round
(claude/accuracy) and the Blower Door Air Seal calculator (claude/blower-door), merged, with the
blower door brought under the accuracy round's rules. Nothing has been applied to production or
deployed; the go-live steps are in section 6.
