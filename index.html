How to shoot the gable
  • Stand straight in front of the gable end. Shooting from off to the side makes the pitch read steep.
  • Back up and zoom in. Distance cuts down lens distortion.
  • Tap the same edge at all three points, such as the top of the rake trim.
  • Hip roof with no gable? Use a garage or dormer gable with the same pitch, or enter degrees below the reading.

Getting a good overhead image
  • Google Maps or Google Earth in satellite view, looking straight down (not tilted). Screenshot with the scale bar showing, then paste it here with Ctrl+V.
  • For scale, tap both ends of the scale bar, or both ends of an edge you measured with a tape.
  • Trace the outer edge of the roof at the drip edge. Overhangs count.
  • Same pitch everywhere? Trace the whole roof as one section, whatever its shape. Add a section only where the pitch changes, and keep sections from overlapping.

Pitch factors

Factor = √(1 + (rise ÷ 12)²). Multiply a flat footprint by the factor to get the sloped area.
